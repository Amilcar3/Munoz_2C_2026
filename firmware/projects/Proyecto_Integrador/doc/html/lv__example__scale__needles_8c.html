void lv_example_scale_needles ( void )

Two round inner scales driven by looping line and image needles.

@title Round scales with animated needles

Two 150 by 150 scales use LV_SCALE_MODE_ROUND_INNER with a 270 degree range rotated to 135 degrees, 31 ticks, and the range 10 to

  1. The left scale attaches an lv_line needle driven by lv_scale_set_line_needle_value; the right one attaches an lv_image needle (img_hand) driven by lv_scale_set_image_needle_value. Both needles animate between 10 and 40 on an infinite 1000 ms forward and 1000 ms reverse loop.
