void lv_example_style_transform_card ( void )

Rotate and scale a styled profile card using an arc and a slider.

@title Transform a card with arc and slider

Separate styles configure a grid-based card with shadow and rounded corners, a circular avatar with shadow, and a gradient like-button. The helper card_create assembles the card from an avatar image, a name label, and a like-button. Two cards are centered on the active screen; the back one is faded with LV_OPA_50. A large arc and a bottom-aligned slider drive the front card through LV_EVENT_VALUE_CHANGED: the arc writes transform_rotation from lv_arc_get_angle_end * 10, while the slider (range 128 to 300, initial 256) sets transform_scale_x and transform_scale_y.
