6/*
7 * Test: find_scroll_obj should not be affected by child object's
8 * transform rotation when determining scroll direction for ancestors.
9 *
10 * Reproduces the bug where a rotated child causes hor_en/ver_en to be incorrectly accumulated,
11 * preventing the scroll search from reaching the actual scrollable
12 * parent (e.g. a horizontal swiper above a vertical list).
13 *
14 * Widget tree:
15 * screen
16 * └── container (horizontal scrollable, like swiperObj_)
17 * └── list_obj (vertical scrollable, no SCROLL_CHAIN_VER)
18 * └── rotated_obj (transform: rotate 90deg)
19 * └── inner_obj (touch target)
20 *
21 * Without the fix: horizontal swipe on inner_obj -> rotated_obj sets
22 * ver_en=true -> list_obj breaks on no chain_ver -> container not found.
23 *
24 * With the fix: ver_en is reset at list_obj iteration -> container found.
25 */
